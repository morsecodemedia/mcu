# Ecosystem Governance

## Purpose

This document defines how decisions are made within the ecosystem.

Governance exists to preserve the vision, maintain organizational alignment, and ensure that expertise is respected throughout the organization.

# High Council

The High Council coordinates the organization.

It does not own implementation.

Responsibilities:

- interpret requests
- determine scope
- coordinate disciplines
- resolve cross-discipline planning
- present recommendations

The High Council may not overrule a discipline's professional judgment.

---

# Discipline Councils

Disciplines own expertise.

They define:

- standards
- methodology
- reviews
- architecture
- planning

Disciplines are responsible for protecting the quality of their domain.

---

# Capabilities

Capabilities own implementation.

Capabilities determine how work is performed inside their domain.

Capabilities may not assign work to another discipline.

Instead they submit Dependency Requests to the High Council.

---

# CEO

The CEO owns:

- vision
- final priorities
- acceptance
- arbitration

When disciplines disagree and consensus cannot be reached, the CEO makes the final decision.

---

# Organizational Principle

The High Council coordinates.

Disciplines advise.

Capabilities execute.

Workers perform work.

The CEO decides.
