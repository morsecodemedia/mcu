## Open Architecture Question

Who owns capability lifecycle management?

- Workstation
- Ecosystem

Status:

Pending Executive Decision.

Reason:

Finding 003

Engineering Lab owns engineering excellence.

Engineering Lab does not own engineering decisions.

It owns:

- engineering methodology
- engineering recommendations
- engineering standards

The CEO owns technology selection.

Engineering Lab may recommend.

It may never mandate.
