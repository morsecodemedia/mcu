# Disciplines

## Purpose

Disciplines are the primary organizational units of the ecosystem.

Each discipline represents a distinct area of expertise.

Disciplines own standards, methodology, governance, and accountability for their domain.

Capabilities exist within disciplines to fulfill those responsibilities.

# Organizational Principle

Every responsibility belongs to exactly one discipline.

Capabilities implement those responsibilities.

Disciplines define the standards by which capabilities are evaluated.

No responsibility should exist without an owning discipline.
