# Ecosystem Organization

## Purpose

The ecosystem is organized as a digital organization.

It exists to amplify human capability by providing specialized disciplines that collaborate toward shared goals.

The ecosystem is not a collection of tools.

It is an organization composed of independent disciplines, each responsible for a specific area of expertise.

---

# Leadership

## CEO

The human user.

The CEO defines:

- vision
- priorities
- goals
- final approval

The CEO remains responsible for all final decisions.

---

## High Council

The High Council is responsible for ecosystem-wide governance.

Responsibilities include:

- interpreting requests
- determining scope
- identifying affected disciplines
- coordinating cross-discipline initiatives
- reviewing completed initiatives
- presenting recommendations to the CEO

The High Council does not perform implementation work.

---

# Disciplines

Each discipline represents an area of expertise.

Examples include:

- Creative
- Technical
- Writing
- Strategy
- Operations

Each discipline owns:

- standards
- methodology
- architecture
- planning
- reviews

Each discipline may contain one or more capabilities.

---

# Capabilities

Capabilities are the operational units of the ecosystem.

Examples:

- Image Lab
- Engineering Agent

Capabilities:

- provide independent value
- own their own implementation
- own their own repositories
- own their own lifecycle

Capabilities should never become dependent upon unrelated capabilities.

---

# Collaboration

Capabilities never delegate work directly to another discipline.

Instead they submit dependency requests to the High Council.

The High Council determines whether:

- additional disciplines are required
- scope should change
- new work should be created

This preserves discipline independence.

---

# Human Oversight

The ecosystem exists to support the CEO.

It does not replace leadership.

Final approval always belongs to the CEO.

The organization recommends.

The CEO decides.
