# MCU Doctrine

## Purpose

The MCU Doctrine captures the enduring architectural principles that guide the evolution of the MorseCodeUniverse.

Where the Constitution defines what the MCU is, the Doctrine defines how the MCU thinks.

The Doctrine is intentionally concise and should remain stable over time.

---

# Principles

## 1. Organizational Architecture

The MCU is a digital consulting organization whose architecture is defined by responsibilities rather than technologies.

---

## 2. Deterministic Before Probabilistic

Deterministic knowledge establishes organizational truth.

Probabilistic systems provide recommendations.

---

## 3. Responsibilities Outlive Implementations

Responsibilities are constitutional.

Implementations are evolutionary.

---

## 4. Independent Organizational Capabilities

Repositories collaborate through explicit interfaces while maintaining independent ownership.

---

## 5. Intent Over Models

Users express intent.

The organization determines implementation.

---

## 6. Documentation Mirrors Architecture

Documentation ownership follows architectural ownership.

---

## 7. Small, Reversible Architecture

Architecture evolves through validated, incremental change.
